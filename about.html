Hello, I’m Lynh

Let’s start with
what matters to you.

My vision for wedding design and planning is rooted in a simple idea: a celebration should feel like the people at the heart of it.

I’m drawn to a softer kind of elegance. The movement of a garden, the warmth of natural textures, the way candlelight changes a room. Not more for the sake of more, but the right details, thoughtfully chosen.

That’s the spirit I want to bring to your day. A beautiful setting, a considered plan, and space for the unscripted moments you’ll want to hold onto.

Warmly, Lynh

01 / Intention

Meaning over more.

The details that belong are the ones that mean something to you. Let’s leave room for what matters and let the rest fall away.

02 / Connection

People at the center.

A wedding is a gathering before it’s anything else. The goal is a space where you and your favorite people can feel completely at home.

03 / A natural balance

Refined, never rigid.

A little structure, a little wildness. Organic shapes and considered details can sit beautifully together, without feeling overdone.
